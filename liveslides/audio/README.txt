Background music for the title-slide countdown billboard.

TO USE:
  1. Drop an .mp3 or .m4a file in this folder.
  2. Open liveslides/index.html and find the "waitingRoom" block near the top.
  3. Set:   music: "audio/your-file-name.mp3"
     (leave it as ""  for no music)
  4. Optional: musicVolume goes from 0 to 1. 0.35 is background level.

It starts when you click "Start 2:00", fades up over 2 seconds, and fades out
during the closing countdown so it isn't competing with "Let's begin".
A small note icon appears bottom-left while it plays - click it to mute.

IMPORTANT - SHARING THE SOUND ON A CALL:
  On Zoom or Teams, browser audio does NOT reach attendees unless you tick
  "Share sound" when you start the screen share. Without it, only you hear it.

Pick something you have the right to play publicly - royalty-free / licensed.
A 2-minute (or looping) calm instrumental works best; it loops automatically.
