3. Your Full Bio & Detail

This is the expanded view that opens when someone taps your card.

Dr. Ariel Steinberger

Cosmetic Dentistry

Dr. Ariel Steinberger earned his Bachelor of Science in Psychology, graduating with honors from Touro College, before receiving his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Touro College of Dental Medicine. During dental school, he was awarded the prestigious American Academy of Esthetic Dentistry Award of Merit in recognition of his outstanding achievement and excellence in cosmetic dentistry. He went on to complete a postdoctoral residency at NYU Langone, followed by hundreds of hours of continuing education in cosmetic and complex restorative dentistry, where he further refined his clinical skills and deepened his commitment to comprehensive, patient-centered care.

He is passionate about combining artistry, technology, and evidence-based dentistry to create beautiful, functional, and lasting smiles. Through his background in psychology and comprehensive treatment planning, Dr. Ariel has developed a particular interest in helping patients understand complex treatment options, build confidence in their decisions, and achieve high levels of case acceptance through ethical, patient-centered communication.
