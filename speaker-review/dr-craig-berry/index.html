3. Your Full Bio & Detail

This is the expanded view that opens when someone taps your card.

Dr. Craig Berry

Endodontics

Dr. Craig Berry is a board-certified endodontist dedicated to giving every patient a comfortable, positive root canal experience — his goal is for patients to leave with a smile, looking forward to the next stage of their treatment. He attended Yeshiva University, graduating cum laude, and earned his dental degree at UMDNJ, finishing at the top of his class. He completed a general practice residency at Woodhull Medical Center in Brooklyn, focusing on comprehensive and complex cases, followed by an endodontic residency at UMDNJ — the only endodontic program in New Jersey — where he trained under some of the country's most respected endodontists using the newest technology. Dr. Berry became board certified within a year of graduating his residency, the highest honor in the field and one held by fewer than 20% of endodontists worldwide, making him one of the youngest endodontists ever to earn it. He maintains membership in the American Association of Endodontists College of Diplomates, the American Dental Association, the New York State Dental Association, and the Nassau County Dental Society, and serves as a clinical attending physician at Lutheran Medical Center, helping shape the next generation of endodontists.
