3. Your Full Bio & Detail

This is the expanded view that opens when someone taps your card.

Dr. Daniel Greenbaum

Prosthodontics & Implantology

Dr. Daniel Greenbaum is a Board-Certified Prosthodontist with Advanced Fellowship Training in Implantology and Periodontology. Originally from Rockland County, NY, he completed his undergraduate studies at CUNY Queens College (summa cum laude, 2003) and received his DDS from Columbia University in 2007. He completed a General Practice Residency at Montefiore Medical Center, specialty training in Prosthodontics at the University of Maryland (MS, 2011), and an additional Fellowship in Implantology and Periodontology (2012). Since then, Dr. Greenbaum has maintained an active private practice, taught at dental schools and study clubs, and participated in multiple dental education organizations.
