3. Your Full Bio & Detail

This is the expanded view that opens when someone taps your card.

Dr. Harold Katz

Oral Health & Product Development

Dr. Harold Katz is the developer of the TheraBreath line of oral products and is an internationally recognized expert in the fields of bad breath, taste disorders, tonsil stones, and dry mouth. After co-founding the California Breath Clinics in 1994 with his brother Dr. Richard Katz, Harold went on to focus on the creation of several efficacious formulas, with one of his daughters as “patient number one”.

Dr. Katz, a graduate of the UCLA School of Dentistry, with a separate degree in Bacteriology, is often referred to as The Bad Breath Guru, through thousands of media appearances not only in the US, but also in dozens of other countries.

His discovery that bad breath is caused by sulfur producing anaerobic bacteria – not the digestive system – led to the initial success of the TheraBreath brand. Dr. Katz sold the brand to Church and Dwight at the end of 2021. TheraBreath, the fastest growing oral care brand in the US, is now the #1 mouthwash brand on Amazon, Target, Walmart, etc.
