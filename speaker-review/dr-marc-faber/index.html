Dr. Marc Faber is a graduate of New York University Dental School and completed his residency at Columbia University, Harlem Hospital Center in New York City. Clinically, the group is heavily focused on incorporating digital technology to provide better, faster, and more affordable dentistry. Culturally, it is focused on creating careers for its 60+ employees by providing continuing education and a framework for growth. Its goal is to grow responsibly into a more efficient, more profitable dental organization within the greater New York area.
