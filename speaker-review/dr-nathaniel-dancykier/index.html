3. Your Full Bio & Detail

This is the expanded view that opens when someone taps your card.

Dr. Nathaniel Dancykier

Prosthodontics & Cosmetic Dentistry

Dr. Nathaniel Dancykier is a prosthodontist specializing in the restoration and replacement of teeth, pairing cutting-edge technology with a deeply personal approach to deliver excellent aesthetic results. He earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree at the University of Maryland Dental School and completed his residency in prosthodontics at the University of Illinois at Chicago. He received the Excellence in Prosthodontics award from the Southeastern Academy of Prosthodontics and was recognized by the American College of Prosthodontists for an award-winning presentation on innovative research in the field. An active member of the American College of Prosthodontists, the American Prosthodontic Society, the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, and Alpha Omega, he recently joined the Towson, Maryland practice of Dr. Keith Boenning. "My goal is always to treat each patient in a comprehensive manner," he says. "I want my patients to leave my office thinking that was the most thorough exam they ever had." Born and raised in Rockland County, New York, Dr. Dancykier is glad to have returned to Maryland with his wife and four children, where he enjoys running, spending time with family, and being active in his community.
