3. Your Full Bio & Detail

The expanded view that opens when someone taps your card.

Dr. Ruth Abramowitz

General & Public Health Dentistry

Brooklyn born-and-bred, Dr. Ruth Abramowitz attended Brooklyn College–CUNY, and obtained a bachelor’s degree in Psychology with a minor in Chemistry. After graduating, Dr. Abramowitz and her family went on an adventure to Arizona, where she obtained her dental degree at the Arizona School of Dentistry and Oral Health (ASDOH) with a certification in Public Health Dentistry from A.T. Still University. She greatly enjoyed her time in Arizona, and continued to achieve a certificate in Advanced Education in General Dentistry through NYU Langone at ASDOH as well.

Ruth’s background with art started well before any consideration of dentistry as a career. From the age of six, Dr. Abramowitz attended painting lessons and advanced through her art school to become an instructor for both acrylic and oil painting. Upon moving to Arizona, she was an art teacher at the local Jewish elementary school while completing her requirements for dental school.

With a great focus on detail as well as intensive training with modern dental technology, Dr. Abramowitz returned to Brooklyn to provide comprehensive care to her community and worked with her father, who is also a dentist, for several years. After moving with her family to Highland Park, NJ, Ruth and her husband became owners of Park Dental Group, where she has continued to provide care to her community, particularly with general dentistry and TMJ pain.

Dr. Abramowitz holds membership with the AGD, AAFE, and the Raritan Valley Study Club, and is actively involved in learning what she can from the other amazing dentists in the Dental Wisdom WhatsApp family.

In her free time, Dr. Ruth enjoys spending time with her family (and her dog too!), participating in her local cookbook club, and enjoying the outdoors.
