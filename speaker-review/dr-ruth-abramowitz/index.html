3. Your Full Bio & Detail Draft

The expanded view that opens when someone taps your card.

Dr. Ruth Abramowitz

General & Public Health Dentistry

Brooklyn born-and-bred, Dr. Ruth Abramowitz attended Brooklyn College–CUNY, and obtained a bachelor’s degree in Psychology with a minor in Chemistry. After graduating, Dr. Abramowitz and her family went on an adventure to Arizona, where she obtained her dental degree at the Arizona School of Dentistry and Oral Health (ASDOH) with a certification in Public Health Dentistry. She greatly enjoyed her time in Arizona, and continued to achieve a certificate in Advanced Education in General Dentistry through NYU at ASDOH as well.

With a focus on helping the underserved, as well as intensive training with modern dental technology, Dr. Abramowitz returned to Brooklyn to provide comprehensive care to her community.

In her free time, Dr. Ruth enjoys spending time with her family (and her dog too!), listening to music, and enjoying the outdoors.

Dr. Abramowitz looks forward to helping you achieve your oral health and dental goals!
