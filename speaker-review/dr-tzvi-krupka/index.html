3. Your Full Bio & Detail

This is the expanded view that opens when someone taps your card.

Dr. Tzvi Krupka

Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery

Dr. Tzvi Krupka is a board certified Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeon at The Hershman Group in Brooklyn, NY and is a founding surgeon at Lakewood Oral Maxillofacial Surgery in Lakewood, NJ. He earned a Bachelor of Science in Biology from Touro University, graduating summa cum laude. He then attended the University of Maryland School of Dentistry, graduating at the top of his class with honors, and was inducted into the Omicron Kappa Upsilon Honor Society. He went on to complete additional training in Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery at Westchester Medical Center and The Maria Fareri Children's Hospital, where he had the honor of serving as chief resident. Dr. Krupka received extensive training in corrective jaw surgery, pathology, craniofacial surgery and facial reconstruction. He is an attending surgeon at Lenox Hill Hospital, St. Joseph's University Medical Center and Westchester Medical Center, where he routinely operates and trains surgical residents. He is also a member of the Westchester Medical Center Cleft Lip and Palate team. Dr. Krupka maintains an active office-based oral surgery practice in Brooklyn, NY and Lakewood, NJ, with a focus on dentoalveolar surgery, wisdom teeth removal, dental implant placement, and complex bone grafting procedures.
