6. Dr. Seth Calish's Bio

This is the pop-up that opens from his card — and your logo is credited at the bottom as the session sponsor.

Dr. Seth Calish

General & Cosmetic Dentistry

Dr. Seth Calish is an accomplished general dentist, multi-practice owner, speaker, and clinical educator based in Central Florida. With advanced training in cosmetic, aesthetic, restorative, and implant dentistry, his clinical practice focuses on comprehensive patient care, full-mouth rehabilitation, and the integration of orthodontics into modern general dentistry.

Dr. Calish earned his Bachelor of Science in Biological Sciences from Florida State University, followed by a master's degree in Medical Sciences from the University of South Florida's Morsani College of Medicine. He received his Doctor of Dental Medicine from Lake Erie College of Osteopathic Medicine, where he was recognized with the Surgeon of the Year Award from the American Academy of Oral Maxillofacial Surgeons.

Passionate about advancing the dental profession, Dr. Calish is actively involved in professional education, lecturing, curriculum development, and clinician mentorship. He serves as President of the Central Florida Academy of General Dentistry and holds fellowships with the American Academy of Implant Prosthodontics, the International Congress of Oral Implantology, and Live Implant Training.

As a clinical educator and advocate for orthobrain, Dr. Calish brings a comprehensive, practice-based perspective to orthodontic integration. His educational expertise spans clear aligner therapy, case selection, biomechanics, clinical workflows, and patient communication, with an emphasis on helping general dentists expand their clinical capabilities and confidently incorporate orthodontics into comprehensive treatment planning.

Drawing on his experience as both a practicing clinician and business owner, Dr. Calish is passionate about empowering dental professionals with practical, actionable strategies that elevate patient care, strengthen clinical confidence, and drive sustainable practice growth.
