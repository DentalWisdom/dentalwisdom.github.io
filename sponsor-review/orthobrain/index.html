Dr. Sam Glick

General & Cosmetic Dentistry

Dr. Sam Glick founded Cleveland Smile Center in 2018. He comes from a long lineage of dentists, including his father, uncle, and grandfather. As CEO of Cleveland Smile Center, he manages four private practices around the suburbs of Cleveland, Ohio. Dr. Glick has achieved advanced education in oral surgery, including dental implantology and total mouth reconstruction with Full Mouth All-On-4 Dental Implants. He also has a special interest in providing orthodontic treatment for both children and adults, helping them benefit from beautiful, straight smiles with both clear aligners and traditional straight wire orthodontics. Dr. Glick helps to provide world-class care with his dedication to cosmetic dentistry. Additionally, Dr. Glick is certified through the American Academy of Facial Esthetics to administer Botox and dermal fillers for those desiring cosmetic treatment beyond just their teeth. Currently, Dr. Glick is pursuing a Fellowship in the Academy of General Dentistry. Outside of work, Dr. Glick enjoys spending time with his wife Stephanie and their two children, Sloan and Silas. Dr. Glick also focuses much of his free time on physical fitness including yoga, strength training, basketball, running and scuba diving.
